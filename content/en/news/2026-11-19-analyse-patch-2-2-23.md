Patch 2.2.23, dated November 11 on the site, reworks Popol and Kupa and adjusts thirteen heroes: six buffed, three nerfed and four changed with no clear direction. The developers are going after a trend in high-rank games, fighters in the Gold Lane and roam tanks in the EXP Lane, and some of the system changes are there to send everyone back to their own lane.

The current rates quoted here were recorded on November 19, 2026, across all ranks unless stated otherwise. The changes compare the site's daily history on October 28 and on November 17, its latest day. These curves are smoothed, and several heroes were already trending the same way before November 11, so the patch's effect can't be isolated down to the exact point. The overall signal is still clear: the fourteen heroes in the patch moved by 1.25 win rate points on average, against 0.24 for the other 119. The five biggest gains and the five biggest drops among the 133 heroes tracked all belong to heroes from the patch.

## The winners

**Aldous** is the big winner. His Skill 2 cooldown goes from 12-9.5 seconds to a flat 8 seconds at every level, and his damage reduction goes from a flat 30% to 30-55%. His win rate climbs from 51.7% to 53.7%, taking him from A to S tier, rises to 54.6% in Mythic, and he's banned in only 0.7% of games.

**Xavier** sees his Mystic Field damage doubled (from 220-345 to 440-690, with the magic power ratio raised from 65% to 130%), in exchange for Mystic Barrier losing its direct damage. He goes from 51.7% to 53.1% (A to S) and his pick rate almost doubles, from 0.71% to 1.25% of games. He sits at 53.6% in Mythical Glory, but only 51.6% in Mythic.

**Lunox** gains 10% spell vamp or magic penetration depending on her passive's state, her Skill 2 hits harder in the early game, and her ultimate trades base damage (250-450 down to 150-300) for scaling (100% up to 135%). Her win rate goes from 50.6% to 52.1% and her pick rate from 0.37% to 0.62%. She stays in A tier because hardly anyone bans her (1.5%).

**Melissa** gets back part of what the previous patch took from her: her Skill 2 damage transfer goes from a flat 52% to 50-60%. She goes from 50.1% to 51.3% (B to A). The gap between ranks is wide: 49.2% in Mythic, 52.6% in Mythical Glory.

**Freya** is listed as "buffed", but her ultimate comes back less often (cooldown from 44-28 to 60-44 seconds) and her basic attacks no longer heal during it. In return, her empowered attacks hit harder. Her win rate barely moves (49.3% to 50.3%), but her ban rate doubles, from 4.4% to 9.5%.

**Kaja** got a mobility buff but isn't winning more (49.8%, then 49.6%). He is, however, banned more: from 15.7% to 20.1%, and 31.5% in Mythical Glory. Those bans are the only reason he's rated S.

## The losers

**Masha** is nerfed across almost her entire kit: her passive's percentage HP damage is reduced, her passive healing drops from 4-10% to 3-6% of max HP, and her Skill 2 damage goes from 120-240 (+80% extra physical attack) to 60-240 (+40%). Her win rate falls from 60.6% to 58.4%. Even so, she's still the top-ranked hero on the site, with a 41.6% ban rate, and 68.9% in Mythical Glory.

**Gloo** loses crowd control (the immobilize on his Skill 1 goes from 0.8 to 0.6 seconds) and durability (the damage transfer on his Skill 3 goes from 80% to 60%). His win rate drops from 53.4% to 51.3%, but a 40.4% ban rate keeps him in S+ tier. In Mythical Glory, he's already below 50% (49.7%).

**Lukas** gets a different profile: more base damage, less physical attack scaling (his Skill 1 goes from 120% to 60% on the main hit), and a bit more HP and defense. His win rate slips from 52.0% to 50.7% and his ban rate from 32.8% to 25.6%. The numbers don't tell us whether the tankier version the developers were aiming for has replaced the damage version.

**Atlas** freezes less often (passive duration from 5 to 4 seconds, freeze cooldown from 2.5 to 4 seconds), in exchange for a little more defense. He drops from 51.4% to 50.0%, from S to A tier. He stays S tier in Mythic (52.2%).

**Popol and Kupa**, reworked, fall from 51.9% to 49.4% (A to B). They bottomed out on November 11 (48.6%) and the curve has been creeping back up since. They're at 50.6% in Mythic.

That leaves three heroes who barely moved. **Karrie** (weaker Skill 1, stronger ultimate) goes from 48.9% to 49.0%. **Kagura** only gets a visual change, and **Uranus**, whose area damage becomes magic damage, is picked in 0.29% of games, below the 0.3% threshold under which the site considers the data unreliable.

## Items and systems

- **Chastise Pauldron** is nerfed: the attacker's attack speed is now reduced to 80% instead of 75%. **Antique Cuirass** is buffed: the attacker's physical damage reduction goes from 6% to 8%. Chastise is still the answer to attack speed marksmen, but it hits them less hard.
- The **Concussive Blast** talent deals a bit more damage (7% to 8% of max HP), but 50% less to minions. This is the change aimed at tanks in the EXP Lane.
- **Sprint** cooldown goes from 100 to 75 seconds.
- On the "Mythical Honor" battlefield (the notes don't say which ranks it applies to), the gold bonus from the Gold Lane siege minion goes from 40% to 60%, to help marksmen farm.
- **Golden Turret**: the base turret gains 2,000 HP instead of 1,000, and the outer and inner turrets give less team gold (100 to 90, 140 to 120). **Healing Turtle**: killing the enemy turtle gives significantly more gold and EXP, though the notes give no exact figure.

The site doesn't track item usage before and after the patch, so the effect of these changes isn't quantified here.

## What changes in each lane

**EXP Lane.** Masha is still the priority ban, Aldous has gained the most, Lukas has dropped off and Freya is banned more. As for the developers' goal, the numbers can't settle it: they're measured per hero, not per lane played, and the five tanks whose main lane it is hold steady (49.95%, then 49.91% as a weighted average).

**Gold Lane.** Melissa is up, Karrie hasn't moved, Popol and Kupa are down. The lane's eighteen marksmen are stable on average (50.11%, then 50.15%): the siege minion gold bonus doesn't show up yet in the all-ranks rates.

**Mid Lane.** This is where the buffs show most clearly: Xavier and Lunox are climbing without getting banned.

**Roam.** Gloo and Atlas are down, and Kaja is banned more but isn't winning more. The twelve roam tanks hold steady on average (49.01%, then 48.93%).

**Jungle.** No jungler is adjusted, apart from Freya and Lukas, who also play there, and Popol and Kupa, whom the site lists as junglers first.

## Worth trying in ranked

- **Aldous** in the EXP Lane: 53.7% win rate, 0.7% ban rate.
- **Xavier** in the Mid Lane: 53.1%, almost never banned, and strongest in Mythical Glory (53.6%).
- **Lunox** in the Mid Lane: 52.1%, still rarely picked (0.62% of games).
- **Melissa** in the Gold Lane, from Mythical Honor up: 52.2% in Mythical Honor, 52.6% in Mythical Glory.

A week after the patch, these numbers are still provisional: several curves haven't settled yet, and a rarely played hero like Lunox or Melissa has a shakier rate than a hero who shows up in a lot of games.
