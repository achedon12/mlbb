Patch 2.2.23, dated November 11 on the site, reworks Popol and Kupa and adjusts thirteen heroes: six strengthened, three weakened, four modified without clear direction. The developers are aiming for a trend of high ranking, fighters in gold lane and roaming tanks in experience lane, and part of the system changes serve to send everyone back to their lane.

The current rates cited here are those noted on November 19, 2026, all ranks combined unless otherwise stated. The developments compare the site's daily history to October 28 and November 17, its last day. These curves are smoothed and several heroes were already moving in the same direction before November 11: we cannot isolate the effect of the patch to the nearest point. The overall signal, however, is clear: the fourteen heroes affected moved by an average of 1.25 victory rate points, compared to 0.24 for the 119 others. The five biggest increases and five biggest drops of the 133 heroes measured are all heroes in the patch.

## The winners

**Aldous** is the big beneficiary. His skill 2 goes from a 12-9.5 second cooldown to 8 seconds at all levels, and his 30% damage reduction fixed at 30-55%. It goes from 51.7% to 53.7% of victories, from A to S, rises to 54.6% at Mythic rank and is only banned in 0.7% of games.

**Xavier** sees the damage of his Mystic Field doubled (from 220-345 to 440-690, with a magic power ratio increased from 65% to 130%), in exchange for the removal of direct damage from Mystic Barrier. It goes from 51.7% to 53.1% (A to S) and its selection almost doubles, from 0.71% to 1.25% of games. He is at 53.6% in Mythic Glory, but only 51.6% in Mythic.

**Lunox** gains 10% spell steal or magic penetration depending on the state of his passive, his skill 2 hits harder at the start of the game, and his ultimate exchanges base damage (250-450 to 150-300) for ratio (100% to 135%). It goes from 50.6% to 52.1% of victories and from 0.37% to 0.62% of selection. She remains in A, due to lack of bans (1.5%).

**Melissa** recovers part of what the previous patch took away from her: the damage transfer of her skill 2 goes from 52% fixed to 50-60%. It goes from 50.1% to 51.3% (B to A). The gap between ranks is strong: 49.2% in Mythic, 52.6% in Mythic Glory.

**Freya** is classified as “strengthened” but her ultimate returns less often (recharge from 44-28 to 60-44 seconds) and her basic attacks no longer heal there. In exchange, its empowered attacks hit harder. She gains few victories (49.3% to 50.3%), but her bans double, from 4.4% to 9.5%.

**Kaja**, reinforced in mobility, does not gain more (49.8% then 49.6%), but he is banned more: from 15.7% to 20.1%, and 31.5% in Mythic Glory. It is rated S only because of these bans.

## The losers

**Masha** is weakened on almost her entire kit: passive damage as a percentage of HP reduced, passive healing reduced from 4-10% to 3-6% of max HP, damage from her skill 2 reduced from 120-240 (+80% additional physical attack) to 60-240 (+40%). His win rate drops from 60.6% to 58.4%. However, she remains the highest ranked hero on the site, with 41.6% bans, and 68.9% in Mythic Glory.

**Gloo** loses control (immobilization of his skill 1 from 0.8 to 0.6 seconds) and resistance (transfer of damage from his skill 3 from 80% to 60%). His win rate drops from 53.4% ​​to 51.3%, but he remains S+ thanks to 40.4% bans. In Mythic Glory, it is already below 50% (49.7%).

**Lukas** changes profile: more base damage, less physical attack ratio (his skill 1 goes from 120% to 60% on the main hit), a little more HP and defense. His win rate fell from 52.0% to 50.7% and his bans from 32.8% to 25.6%. The rates do not say whether the defensive version intended by the developers replaced the damage version.

**Atlas** freezes less often (passive duration from 5 to 4 seconds, freeze cooldown from 2.5 to 4 seconds), in exchange for a little more defense. It goes down from 51.4% to 50.0%, from S to A. It remains S at Mythic rank (52.2%).

**Popol and Kupa**, reshuffled, fall from 51.9% to 49.4% (A to B). Their low was on November 11 (48.6%) and the curve has risen slightly since then. They are at 50.6% in Mythic.

Three almost immobile heroes remain. **Karrie** (skill 1 weaker, ultimate stronger) increased from 48.9% to 49.0%. **Kagura** only receives a visual change, and **Uranus**, whose area of ​​effect damage becomes magical, is played in 0.29% of games, below the 0.3% threshold below which the site deems the measurement unreliable.

## Objects and systems

- **Chastise Pauldron** is weakened: the attacker's attack speed is reduced to 80% instead of 75%. **Antique Cuirass** is strengthened: the attacker's physical damage reduction increases from 6% to 8%. Chastise remains the answer to attack speed shooters, but weighs less.
- The **Concussive Blast** talent does a little more damage (7% to 8% of total HP), but 50% less to minions. This is the measure that targets tanks in experience lane.
- **Sprint** reduced from 100 to 75 seconds cooldown.
- On the “Mythical Honor” battlefield (the notes do not specify which ranks it applies to), the gold bonus for the siege minion in gold lane increases from 40% to 60%, to help shooters farm.
- **Golden Turret**: Base turret gains 2,000 HP instead of 1,000, outer and inner turrets give less team gold (100 to 90, 140 to 120). **Healing Turtle**: killing the opposing turtle brings significantly more gold and experience, without a precise number in the notes.

The site does not measure the use of objects before and after the patch: the effect of these changes is not quantified here.

## What does it change by lane

**Experience lane.** Masha remains the priority ban, Aldous has progressed the most, Lukas has fallen back, Freya is banned more. On the developers' objective, the rates do not differ: they are measured per hero, not per lane played, and the five tanks for which this is the main lane remain stable (49.95% then 49.91% on weighted average).

**Gold Lane.** Melissa goes up, Karrie doesn't move, Popol and Kupa back up. The eighteen shooters in the lane are stable on average (50.11% then 50.15%): the gold bonus for the siege minion is not yet seen in the rates for all ranks combined.

**Middle lane.** This is where the reinforcements are most clearly seen: Xavier and Lunox are progressing without being banned.

**Roam.** Gloo and Atlas fall back, Kaja wins in bans but not in victories. The twelve roam tanks remain stable on average (49.01% then 48.93%).

**Jungle.** No junglers are adjusted, apart from Freya and Lukas, who also play there, and Popol and Kupa, who the site first places in jungle.

## To try in ranked

- **Aldous** in experience lane: 53.7% wins, 0.7% bans.
- **Xavier** in the middle: 53.1%, almost never banned, especially effective in Mythic Glory (53.6%).
- **Lunox** in the middle: 52.1%, still little played (0.62% of games).
- **Melissa** in gold lane, from Mythic Honor: 52.2% in Mythic Honor, 52.6% in Mythic Glory.

A week after the patch, these figures remain provisional: several curves have not finished moving, and a little-played hero like Lunox or Melissa has a more fragile rate than a hero present in many games.
