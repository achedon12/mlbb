The site's tier list is not handwritten: it combines each hero's win rate and a quarter of their ban rate. A hero can therefore be at the top because he wins, because he is feared, or both. The numbers below were taken on September 11, 2026, all ranks combined unless otherwise noted, and each hero is counted in their main lane.

Out of 132 heroes measured, 17 are in S+ and 11 in S. Their distribution by lane is very uneven.

## Roam: top half of the rankings

On patch 2.1.88, which strengthened most roamers, thirteen of the twenty-eight heroes classified S+ or S play in roam. There we find the two best win rates in the game, Marcel (58.6%) and Rafaela (58.5%), but also three of the most banned heroes: Belerick (61%), Gloo (49%) and Estes (39%).

The paradox of the lane is elsewhere: the two most played roamers are Tigreal (45.6% wins) and Angela (48.5%), both below average. Belerick, Rafaela and Floryn, who follow in the number of games, are in S+.

**To remember:** in roaming, the most popular choice is not the most effective. Minotaur (54.5%) and Khufra (54.4%) earn more while being banned very rarely.

## Jungle: a classification driven by fear

Five junglers are in S+ or S, but three of them owe it mainly to their ban rate. Hirara, arrived with patch 2.1.88, is banned in 63% of games, and in 81% in Mythic Glory. Hanzo (42% bans) and Saber (27%) are ranked S+ with a win rate around 50%.

Among high-ranked junglers, those earning the most are Barats (53.0%) and Yi Sun-shin (52.0%). Yi Sun-shin is also the most played jungler, ahead of Gusion, whose win rate (48.9%) is significantly lower.

**Keep in mind:** if Hirara and Hanzo are banned, Barats and Yi Sun-shin remain the safe bets, two heroes whose win rate peaks in short games.

## EXP lane: Paquito and Sun, then a tight peloton

Paquito is banned in every other game for a win rate of 50.9%, and Sun combines a good win rate (52.9%) with a third of bans. Masha has the best rate in the lane (56.5%), but in too few games for the figure to be reliable. Behind them, Lukas (53.3%) is the most effective of the heroes who are rarely banned and played enough for the measure to hold.

The most played hero in the lane, Dyrroth, is ranked at Tier A with 50.1% wins; Guinevere and Silvanna, also very well played, are at the same level.

**Keep in mind:** After the Mid lane, the EXP lane is where the win rates are tightest. Aside from the two priority bans, the gap between popular heroes is small.

## Mid lane: Eudora, then no one

Only two mages are classified S+ or S: Eudora, banned in 62% of games, and Valir (52.8% victories, almost never banned). In Mythic Glory, Eudora is even the only heroine in the lane above tier A.

The most played mages after Eudora are Vexana (50.3%), Novaria (48.8%), Nana (47.5%) and Zetian (51.0%): none exceeds tier A.

**Keep in mind:** once Eudora is banished, the Mid lane has no dominant choice. Valir, best win rate in the lane, wins without attracting bans.

## Gold lane: three shooters, then a big gap

Melissa (57.0%), Miya (53.6%) and Hanabi (52.5%) are the only three shooters ranked S+ or S. Miya and Hanabi are the two most played heroes in the game, across all lanes. Behind them in the ranking, no other shooter exceeds 50.6% wins.

Lesley, the fourth most played shooter, is at 46.4%: her victory rate is very low in short games and only exceeds 50% after the eighteenth minute.

**To remember:** the Gold lane is the most concentrated: its two most played heroes are also the most played in the game, and only three shooters exceed tier A.

## What these numbers don't say

A win rate measures how well a hero performs as played, by everyone who chooses him. A hero who is rarely played, like Masha or Marcel, has a more fragile rate than a hero who is present in a lot of games. And a high ban rate signals fear, not necessarily true power: Kaja is ranked S+ with a win rate of 47.6%. To choose, the sheet for each hero details their counters and their builds at your rank.
