> reformulates and comments on them; it does not replace them.

## Weakened heroes

**Khufra** — the recharge of skill 1 increases at the start of the game. THE
hero retains his role of responding to movements, but can no longer do so
offer continuously before level eight. A change measure: it aims to
frequency, not the effect.

**Estes** — ultimate cooldown increases at early levels. The logic
is the same: reduce the number of times he can cancel a fight, without
affect the volume of care per use.

## Heroes strengthened

**Franco** — grappling hook projectile speed increases slightly. This does not
changes nothing for the players who were already touching their grappling hooks; this makes the
hero a little less punitive in learning.

**Pharsa** — ultimate damage increases at later levels. A
attempt to compensate for its vulnerability to jungle assassins, without
touch on its fragility.

## Objects

**Heal reduction** — the effect now applies more quickly after the
first blow hits. In practice, this makes the object significantly more reliable on
intermittent damage carriers, which expands the list of heroes for
which it is worth.

**Ancient Armor** — reduction against consecutive attacks is
slightly reduced. The item remained the default choice for all tanks,
whatever the opposing composition.

## Overall reading

The patch tightens the frequency of skills that decide a fight, rather
than their power. It's a healthier direction: it leaves the heroes
identical to play, but makes the choice of timing more important. The tanks of
roam remain strong, they just require more discipline.
