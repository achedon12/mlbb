This is the most frequent reproach after a defeat: “their Estes took care of
too much ". In almost all cases, no one on the team had purchased
reduction of care.

## The problem, in simple numbers

A regeneration hero doesn't win because he heals a lot. He wins
because its healing exceeds the sustained damage of the opposing team. It's a
threshold, not a curve: either you are above it or you are below it.

Reducing care does nothing other than move this threshold. She doesn't make
not the higher damage, it makes the opposing healing insufficient. On a
Estes, a single item on the team is often enough to turn a fight around
objective that you systematically lose.

## Who should buy it

Not everyone. Only one player, and preferably the one whose damage is
the most continuous, since the effect applies the moment it hits the target.
In practice: the experienced lane fighter or shooter.

The jungler is a bad carrier, because he strikes in spurts: between two
of his commitments, the effect has already dissipated.

## When to buy it

From the second or third object, not last. This is the biggest mistake
common: the item is purchased at minute twenty, while the fights
Determining goal changes took place between the tenth and fifteenth grades.

If the opposing team lines up two regeneration sources — one Estes and one
Yu Zhong, for example — two players must wear them. The effect does not stack
not on the same target, but it guarantees that a carrier is always present
in the fight.

## The reflex to take

Watch the opposing composition on the selection screen, not at the minute
fifteen. If it contains significant healing or regeneration, decide
right away who buys the reduction. This ten second decision is worth more
than most build optimizations.
