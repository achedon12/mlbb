The gold lane is the side lane that yields the most gold. We place the hero who needs it the most to become strong: almost always a [marksman](/heroes/role/marksman), sometimes another damage dealer. His role is summed up in one sentence: survive and farm until his items make him the hero who wins late-game fights.

## What the position brings

- **Constant damage**: Where a mage or assassin hits hard and then waits for his skills, the marksman deals damage with every basic attack. The longer the fight lasts, the more it weighs.
- **Taking turrets**: Basic attacks take down turrets faster than any other role.
- **A late game threat**: a marksman with three or four items forces the opponent to play around him.

On the other hand, the marksman is fragile and often not very mobile. He depends on his team to be protected, and on his lane to be rich. The [farm and wave guide](/guides/farming-wave-management) details this part of the work.

## Most played items, emblems and spells

<!-- guide:kit -->

These choices are a basis, not a rule. A marksman's build changes depending on the opponent:

- **against tanks**, items that deal damage based on the target's HP, like [Demon Hunter Sword](/items/demon-hunter-sword), or that penetrate armor, like [Malefic Gun](/items/malefic-gun);
- **against a team that heals itself**, the healing reduction of [Sea Halberd](/items/sea-halberd);
- **against assassins**, a survival item like [Wind of Nature](/items/wind-of-nature), which briefly protects against physical damage.

On the spell side, [Flicker](/spells/flicker) remains the versatile choice for repositioning or fleeing. [Inspire](/spells/inspire) and [Purify](/spells/purify) meet specific needs: more damage for heroes with basic attacks, or an exit against crowd control that would kill you.

## How a game unfolds

### Early game

The marksman is vulnerable for the first few minutes. His goal is to farm without dying: finish off minions, avoid unfavorable exchanges, and stay close to his turret until the opposing jungler has been seen. The roamer often helps him during the first waves, then leaves to prepare the Turtle.

### Mid-game

With his first items, the marksman begins to weigh. He pushes his lane, takes the outside turret, then joins his team for the nearby objectives. He never crosses the entire map alone.

### Late game

The marksman becomes the opponent's priority target. He places himself behind his front line, strikes the closest target he can safely reach, and never advances into a bush first. The [teamfight guide](/guides/teamfight-positioning) details this placement.

## The most common mistakes

- Join each fight from the first minutes instead of farming.
- Stand in front of your tank to reach a distant target.
- Always buying the same build, regardless of the opponents.
- Farming alone in the enemy jungle at the end of the game, without vision.
- Use the Flicker to attack rather than keeping it to survive.
