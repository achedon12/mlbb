The jungler has no lane. He gains his gold and his experience on the monsters of the jungle, with the spell [Retribution](/spells/retribution), and he is the only one who can secure the Turtle and the Lord against the opposing jungler. The post mainly welcomes [assassins](/heroes/role/assassin) and [fighters](/heroes/role/fighter), sometimes tanks: any hero capable of farming quickly and weighing in from the middle of the game.

## What the position brings

- **The objectives**: Turtle, Lord, buffs. The jungler's Retribution decides who takes them.
- **Rhythm**: the jungler chooses where he shows up, and the lanes adapt their game to his presence or absence.
- **Eliminations**: a jungler who surprises a pushed lane transforms an ally's lead into the team's lead.
- **Often, the team carry**: like the marksman, he becomes one of the richest heroes in the game.

The [farming path and objectives guide](/guides/jungle-objectives) details the times and order of camps.

## Recommended heroes right now

<!-- guide:heroes -->

Junglers are divided into three families. Assassins, such as [Ling](/heroes/ling), [Lancelot](/heroes/lancelot) or [Hayabusa](/heroes/hayabusa), eliminate an isolated target; fighters, like [Martis](/heroes/martis) or [Barats](/heroes/barats), last long fights; Jungle tanks, like [Fredrinn](/heroes/fredrinn) or [Baxia](/heroes/baxia), leave the gold for others and engage. The right choice depends on what your team is missing.

## Most played items, emblems and spells

<!-- guide:kit -->

Retribution is mandatory: without it, farming is too slow and objectives are lost. The rest of the build follows the hero and the opponent:

- **against tanks**, the armor penetration of [Malefic Roar](/items/malefic-roar);
- **against a team that heals itself**, the healing reduction of [Sea Halberd](/items/sea-halberd);
- **to finish a target**, execute items like [Sky Piercer](/items/sky-piercer) on assassins who build them.

[Retribution](/tools/retribution) timing training helps you no longer lose an objective on the last shot.

## How a game unfolds

### Early game

The jungler farms his two buffs and his small camps to reach his ultimate at level 4, then places himself near the Turtle before 2:00. Meanwhile, he looks at the lanes: an opposing lane pushed without vision is an opportunity.

### Mid-game

The jungler alternates farming, objectives and attacks on the lanes. He keeps an eye on enemy buffs: a stolen buff delays the enemy jungler by 90 seconds. The [objective timer](/tools/timer) follows these times in its place.

### Late game

The battles are fought around the Lord. The jungler should not be the first to enter the fight: he waits until the opponent's controls have been used, then he targets the most exposed carry. And he always keeps his Retribution for the Lord.

## The most common mistakes

- Farm your jungle while the teamfights for the Turtle.
- Use Retribution on a small camp just before an objective.
- Attack a lane where the opponent is under his turret with all his HP.
- Open a teamfight first with a fragile assassin.
- Start the Lord without knowing where the adversaries are.
