The exp lane is the side lane where you gain the most experience. Its player spends the start of the game alone, facing a single opponent, which makes it the lane closest to a duel. It mainly welcomes [fighters](/heroes/role/fighter), sometimes tanks: strong heroes, capable of standing alone and joining the battles on the front line.

## What the position brings

- **Experience**: one level ahead means stronger skills and an earlier ultimate.
- **A front line**: in team combat, the exp lane player is often the second strongest hero, after the tank.
- **Pressing the Turtle**: the first Turtle appears near the exp lane, and the player who gains his lane there can help his jungler take it.
- **Split push**: in the middle of the game, a strong hero in a duel can push alone and force the opponent to come and defend him.

The duel is not the main thing. An exp lane won is a wave of advance, a level of advance and an enemy turret started, not necessarily an opponent killed.

## Most played items, emblems and spells

<!-- guide:kit -->

The build adapts to what the opponent does, even more than other positions:

- **against sustained physical damage**, a defense item like [Antique Cuirass](/items/antique-cuirass) or [Blade Armor](/items/blade-armor);
- **against magic damage**, [Athena's Shield](/items/athena-s-shield) or [Radiant Armor](/items/radiant-armor);
- **against a team that heals**, healing reduction, with [Sea Halberd](/items/sea-halberd) or [Dominance Ice](/items/dominance-ice) depending on your hero.

In terms of spells, [Flicker](/spells/flicker) remains the most versatile. [Vengeance](/spells/vengeance) punishes opponents who focus you, and [Petrify](/spells/petrify) gives control to heroes who lack it.

## How a game unfolds

### Early game

The first minutes are played on the wave. Win exchanges when the opponent finishes off a minion or has just launched his main skill, and retreat when your wave is smaller than his. Report each absence of your opponent: he may be joining the Turtle.

### Mid-game

Once the outer turret has fallen, two plans are possible. Either you join your team for the objectives, on the front line; or you push alone to attract an opponent. The [macro and split push guide](/guides/macro-split-push) explains how to choose.

### Late game

In teamfights, the fighter often waits for the opponent's controls to be used before entering. His ideal target is the most exposed opposing carry. If he enters too early, he dies under the controls; if he enters at the right time, he forces the opponent to turn against him.

## The most common mistakes

- Chase a low HP opponent out of the lane, leaving two waves behind.
- Accept an unfavorable duel because you are one level ahead, without looking at where the opposing jungler is.
- Cross the map for an uncertain fight before the first turret falls.
- Ignore the Turtle as it appears right next to his lane.
- Build all your damage items against a team that deals mostly magic damage.
