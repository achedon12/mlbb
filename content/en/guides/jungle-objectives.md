It's the jungler who sets the pace of the game: he chooses where he goes, when he shows up and what objective he takes, and the other four play around these choices. This guide covers the farming path and objectives; The [jungler position guide](/guides/jungle) details heroes and builds.

## Timings to know by heart

All the timings below come from the wiki pages that [objective timer](/tools/timer) cites and dates. They can change with a patch: in case of doubt, the timer takes precedence.

- **Orange Buff**: Appears at 0:20, returns 90 seconds after death.
- **Purple buff**: appears at 0:25, also returns 90 seconds after death.
- **Effect of buffs**: 75 seconds on the hero who wears them.
- **Turtle**: First appearance at 2:00 sharp, near the EXP lane. It returns two minutes after its death, but a Turtle killed after 6:00 no longer returns.
- **Lord**: he appears two minutes after the last Turtle. A Turtle still alive at 8:00 becomes Lord on the spot as soon as it remains 5 seconds without taking damage, and no later than 9:00.

The [map guide](/map) places each camp and recalls what it brings.

## The first route

The starting path has only one goal: to pass level 4 (therefore unlocking the ultimate) with the two buffs, before the Turtle appears. The most common path starts with the buff that corresponds to the hero (orange for heroes who hit with basic attack, purple for those who live off their skills), chain together small nearby camps, then take the other buff.

A few rules apply whatever the path:

- **finish each camp**: a small camp left halfway means lost experience and a delayed return time;
- **secure the little Crab** when he is on your way: his gold buff remains profitable at the start of the game;
- **do not start on the side where your lane is weakest** if the opponent has an invading roamer: you risk losing your first buff.

The key moment of the start of the game comes right after: at 2:00, the Turtle appears, and the jungler who finished his way at 1:55 near the river almost always takes it.

## The Turtle: the early game objective

The Turtle brings gold and experience to the whole team, plus a shield. Three Turtles in a row for a single team makes a gold gap difficult to make up for at the start of the game, without a single kill having taken place.

Before you start, ask yourself three questions:

1. **Where is the opposing jungler?** If he has just appeared on the other side of the map, the Turtle is yours.
2. **Has my neighboring lane grown?** The EXP lane and roamer should be able to reach you in a few seconds.
3. **Do I have my Retribution?** Without it, any opposing jungler can steal the objective at the last moment.

If the answer to the first question is "I don't know", take the Turtle with the team around you, or wait. A stolen Turtle counts double: you lose it, and the opponent takes it.

## The Lord: the objective that ends games

Once killed, the Lord is invoked for your team: he pushes a lane with you, and your next wave of minions is reinforced. This is the objective that helps the most in bringing down the last turrets.

- **Never start it without knowing where at least three opponents are.** A Lord is taken slowly, and a team that arrives right in the middle almost always wins the fight.
- **The best moment is just after a won fight**: two or three opponents dead, and the Lord is safe.
- **One Lord is not enough alone**: regroup and advance with him and your reinforced wave, so that he arrives at the opposing turrets with the whole team.

## Retribution, and the duel of objectives

Retribution inflicts fixed pure damage on a monster: 520 plus 80 per hero level, or 600 at level 1 and 1,720 at level 15. The jungler who casts it first, at the right time, takes the objective. The right time is when the Turtle's or Lord's life points fall under the damage of the spell, and not a second before.

Two reflexes to work on:

- **count your team's damage**: a monster that descends quickly can pass below the threshold between two attacks;
- **do not cast Retribution on a camp just before an objective**: it recharges in 35 seconds, and that is exactly the time during which the opponent can attempt the theft.

[Retribution timing training](/tools/retribution) works precisely on this reflex, on the real HP of each objective.

## After the objectives: buff control

Once the Turtle is taken, the jungler returns to his buffs, then to those of the opponent if the card allows it. Stealing an opposing buff not only rewards the effect: it delays his jungler by 90 seconds. Note the moment when you see an opposing buff die, or use the [objective timer](/tools/timer): you will know when it returns.

## The most common mistakes

- Farm your jungle while the teamfights for the Turtle: the jungler is the only one with Retribution.
- Starting an objective without vision, hoping to go quickly: this is the favorite scenario of the opposing jungler.
- Waste Retribution to finish off a hero or small camp when an objective arrives.
- Let the Lord grow alone while the team farms elsewhere: without an escort, the opponent defends him without difficulty.
