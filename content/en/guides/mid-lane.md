The Mid lane cuts the map in two. This is the shortest lane, and the one from which you can reach both sides the quickest. We most often place a [mage](/heroes/role/mage): a hero who quickly clears the waves, inflicts high magic damage and can join the Turtle, the Gold lane or the EXP lane in a few seconds.

## What the position brings

- **Magic damage**: Mid is often the team's main source of magic damage, and the opponent cannot rely entirely on physical defense.
- **Wave Clearing**: A mage who clears a wave in seconds is free to leave.
- **Rotations**: from the Mid lane, we reach the Turtle, the Lord and the two side lanes faster than from anywhere else.
- **Control**: many mages slow down, immobilize or push back, which prepares the team for engagements.

## Recommended heroes right now

<!-- guide:heroes -->

Not all mages play the same way. Some, like [Yve](/heroes/yve) or [Vexana](/heroes/vexana), control an area and shine in team fights; others, like [Eudora](/heroes/eudora) or [Aurora](/heroes/aurora), explode an isolated target; still others, like [Kagura](/heroes/kagura) or [Harley](/heroes/harley), rely on their mobility. Choose according to what is missing from your composition.

## Most played items, emblems and spells

<!-- guide:kit -->

The mage's build adapts to the team opposite:

- **against heroes who accumulate magic defense**, a penetration item like [Divine Glaive](/items/divine-glaive);
- **against a self-healing team**, [Glowing Wand](/items/glowing-wand), which burns the target and reduces its healing and shields;
- **against divers**, a survival item like [Winter Crown](/items/winter-crown), which freezes you for two seconds: neither targetable nor damageable, but immobile.

The [damage calculator](/tools/damage-calculator) shows the effect of penetration on a real hit. On the spell side, [Flicker](/spells/flicker) remains the most common choice; [Flameshot](/spells/flameshot) strikes from afar to finish off a fleeing target.

## How a game unfolds

### Early game

Clear the wave as quickly as possible, then look at the map. The first minutes of the Mid lane are a constant back and forth: wave, river, wave. If your opponent disappears, report it immediately: he goes to a side lane or to the Turtle.

### Mid-game

The mage spends his time in rotation: he pushes his wave, then joins the objective or the lane where a fight is being prepared. The [guide to rotations and vision](/guides/roaming-vision) details these movements, which the mage often shares with the roamer.

### Late game

In team combat, the mage stays behind his front line and launches his area skills among grouped opponents. He's not looking for the perfect target: a skill thrown at three opponents is better than a skill kept.

## The most common mistakes

- Leave the Mid lane without having cleaned the wave: the mid turret falls quickly, and its fall opens the jungle on both sides.
- Drain all your skills on a wave while an opponent may arrive.
- Stand in front of your tank to reach a target.
- Ignore the enemy's magic defense and never build penetration.
- Stay in lane while the Turtle argues next to it.
