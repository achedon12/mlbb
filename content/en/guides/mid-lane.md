The mid lane cuts the map in two. This is the shortest lane, and the one from which you reach both sides of the map the quickest. We most often place a [mage](/heroes/role/mage): a hero who quickly clears the waves, inflicts high magic damage and can join the Turtle, the gold lane or the exp lane in a few seconds.

## What the position brings

- **Magic damage**: often the team's main source of magic damage, which prevents the opponent from relying entirely on armor.
- **Wave Clearing**: A mage who clears a wave in seconds is free to leave.
- **Rotations**: from the mid lane, we reach the Turtle, the Lord and the two side lanes faster than from anywhere else.
- **Control**: many mages slow down, immobilize or push back, which prepares the team for engagements.

## Recommended heroes right now

<!-- guide:heroes -->

Not all mages play the same way. Some, like [Yve](/heroes/yve) or [Vexana](/heroes/vexana), control an area and shine in teamfights; others, like [Eudora](/heroes/eudora) or [Aurora](/heroes/aurora), hit a single target very hard; still others, like [Kagura](/heroes/kagura) or [Harley](/heroes/harley), rely on their mobility. Choose according to what is missing from your composition.

## Most played items, emblems and spells

<!-- guide:kit -->

The mage's build follows the opponent's magic defense:

- **against heroes who accumulate magic defense**, a penetration item like [Divine Glaive](/items/divine-glaive);
- **against a team that heals**, the healing reduction of [Necklace of Durance](/items/necklace-of-durance);
- **against divers**, a survival item like [Winter Crown](/items/winter-crown), which briefly makes you untouchable.

The [damage calculator](/tools/damage-calculator) shows the effect of penetration on a real hit. On the spell side, [Flicker](/spells/flicker) remains the most common choice; [Flameshot](/spells/flameshot) strikes from afar to finish off a fleeing target.

## How a game unfolds

### Early game

Clear the wave as quickly as possible, then look at the map. The first minutes of the mid lane are a constant back and forth: wave, river, wave. If your opponent disappears, report it immediately: he is going towards a side lane or towards the Turtle.

### Mid-game

The mage becomes a rotation player. He pushes his wave, then joins the objective or the lane where a fight is being prepared. The [guide to rotations and vision](/guides/roaming-vision) details these movements, which the mage often shares with the roamer.

### Late game

In team combat, the mage stays behind his front line and launches his area skills among grouped opponents. He's not looking for the perfect target: a skill thrown at three opponents is better than a skill kept.

## The most common mistakes

- Leave the mid lane without having cleared the wave: the central turret falls quickly, and it opens the jungle on both sides.
- Launch all your skills on a wave when an opponent may arrive.
- Stand in front of your tank to reach a target.
- Ignore the enemy's magic defense and never build penetration.
- Stay in lane while the Turtle argues next to it.
