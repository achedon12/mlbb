In a team fight, each hero has a place. When everyone holds their own, the fight goes as planned; when only one player deviates, the whole team often loses. Positioning is not a matter of reflexes: it is decided before the start of the fight.

## The three lines of a team

Any fight can be read as three successive lines.

- **The front line**: the tank and often the fighter of the EXP lane. They absorb, engage and protect. They must be in front, but not alone.
- **The damage line**: the shooter and the mage. They deal most of the damage in combat, provided you survive. They strike whatever is within range, without moving forward to hit a distant target.
- **The divers**: the assassin and certain fighters. They go around the front line to reach the opposing carriers. The important thing is not to arrive first, but at the right time.

A lost fight often comes down to a line that has left its place: a shooter in front of his tank, a tank who engages while his team is still far away, an assassin who dives before the opponent has used his controls.

## Before the fight: choose to fight

Sometimes the best decision is not to fight. Before accepting a fight, check three things:

1. **The numbers**: four against five, a balanced fight becomes a losing fight. Wait for the missing player, or move back.
2. **Ultimates**: an opposing tank without an ultimate can no longer engage. It's time to move forward.
3. **The objective**: a fight that does not bring in a Turtle, a Lord, or a turret is only worth it if it is very favorable.

The [team analysis](/tools/team) helps you see before the game what your composition can do: engage, protect, last, or hit hard and fast.

## The front line: engage at the right time

The tank decides when the fight begins. A good engagement meets three conditions: the team is close enough to follow, the target is an opposing carry or a tight group, and allied damage skills are available.

- An engagement of [Tigreal](/heroes/tigreal) or [Atlas](/heroes/atlas) only has value if damage follows in the coming seconds.
- A tank that protects rather than engages stays close to its carriers and awaits the arrival of opposing divers.
- The EXP lane fighter does the opposite of the tank: he waits until the opponent has used his controls, then he enters.

## The damage line: hit what is in range

The marksman and mage must inflict as much damage as possible, for as long as possible. The most useful rule: **hit the closest target you can safely reach**, even if it's the tank.

- Moving forward to reach the opposing marksman means going in front of your own front line.
- A mage who casts his area skills in the middle of a group is worth more than a mage who waits for the perfect target.
- Against a team full of divers, a defensive item like [Wind of Nature](/items/wind-of-nature) (immunity to physical damage) or [Winter Crown](/items/winter-crown) (two seconds of invulnerability) saves you the few seconds that decide the fight.

The [damage calculator](/tools/damage-calculator) shows what each hit inflicts after defense and penetration: enough to understand why hitting a tank without a penetration item leads nowhere.

## Divers: arriving at the right time

The assassin must not open the fight. He waits until the opposing front line is occupied and the opposing controls have left, then he enters the most exposed carrier. A diver who arrives too early dies under control; at the right moment, he decides the fight in a second.

## Who to target

The safest order of priority:

1. the most exposed opposing carry, the one that can be reached without crossing the front line;
2. the hero who heals or protects, if he is within range;
3. the rest, starting with the closest one.

Against a team that heals a lot, healing reduction items change the outcome of long fights: [Sea Halberd](/items/sea-halberd) for physical damage, [Glowing Wand](/items/glowing-wand) for magic damage, [Dominance Ice](/items/dominance-ice) for a tank. The [item catalog](/items) indicates who builds them.

## The most common mistakes

- Fighting four against five because one opponent is almost out of HP.
- Engage with the tank while allied carries are still farming.
- A marksman who positions himself in front of his tank to hit a distant target.
- Pursue an opponent who is fleeing instead of taking the objective that the won fight has just opened.
