# FAQ

**Can I see my match statistics?**
Only when Moonton lets them through. Once you sign in with your game account,
the account page and the player profile show your figures, recent matches and
heroes played, but only while Moonton's battle report service answers, and it
is often offline. The site has no other way to get them.

**Is the data up to date?**
It is refreshed by the sync from the wiki and the community API: measurements
every night, the full catalogue every Monday. The date of the last sync is shown
on the site.

**Why are some texts translated imperfectly?**
Content such as lore and skills is translated automatically. Proper names and
game terms can come out approximate; corrections are welcome.

**Is the site official?**
No. It is an independent fan project. *Mobile Legends: Bang Bang* is a trademark
of Shanghai Moonton Technology Co., Ltd.

**Which data depends on an external site at runtime?**
None of the content: all data and images are copied into the repository. Only
the signed-in account area talks to Moonton live, through the community API.
