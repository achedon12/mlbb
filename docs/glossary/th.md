# Thai (th) game glossary

Thai players read Thai sentences with the game's words in English or in
their usual transliteration: ฮีโร่, สกิน, บิลด์, สกิล, เคาน์เตอร์, and
English for the rest (Tier List, Win Rate, roles, lanes, ranks). The machine
translation turns the game words into dictionary Thai: รถถัง (a tank, the
vehicle), ผิวหนัง (skin, the organ), ตำนานมือถือ (Mobile Legends, word for
word).

Thai has no spaces between words: a Latin word gets a space on each side
("เครื่องคำนวณ Win Rate"). The glossary rules (`scripts/translation-glossary.mjs`,
`th`) only list long, unambiguous Thai terms, matched without word
boundaries, and add those spaces.

Dates: the Thai calendar counts years in the Buddhist era (2569 for 2026);
the site counts in Gregorian years everywhere (`LOCALE_INTL`).

talent names, MPL, MSC, MVP.
